This font is bundled in the app only to draw the book titles on the
archive-style 3D shelf and under it (serif / 宋体 look). It is a
subset covering GB2312 (6763 hanzi), ASCII and common punctuation,
produced with fontTools; glyph outlines are not otherwise modified.
Rare characters outside the subset fall back to the system font.
